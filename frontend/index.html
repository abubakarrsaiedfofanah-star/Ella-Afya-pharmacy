QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know before you sign in.

How registration, role access and the installed app work.

Who can register for a Sales account?+

Sales registration is for invited pharmacy staff. Your account remains pending until an administrator approves it. Ask your administrator for the registration key.

Can I register an Admin account here?+

No. Admin access is provisioned separately and is limited to the pharmacy’s authorized administrator.

How do I reset my password?+

Use the password reset page. Sales password reset · Admin password reset.

Can Sales staff access Admin tools?+

No. Sales and Admin use separate sign-in pages, and your account role determines which workspace and actions are available.

Does installing the app let me work offline?+

The app can keep static interface files available. Signing in and working with live stock, prescriptions, sales and payments still requires an internet connection.
