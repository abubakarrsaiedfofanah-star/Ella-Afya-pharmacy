COMMON QUESTIONS

Before your visit.

A few quick answers about medicines, prescriptions and payment.

Do I need a prescription for every medicine?+

Some medicines require a valid prescription. Bring yours if the medicine is prescription-only; the pharmacy team can guide you.

Can you confirm if a medicine is available?+

Availability can change. Ask the pharmacy team to confirm whether a medicine is currently in stock.

What payment methods can I use?+

Payments can be made by cash or M-Pesa PayBill at the counter. Staff confirm M-Pesa payments against the transaction details.

Can I order or pay through this website?+

This public page provides information only. It does not place orders or accept payments online.
